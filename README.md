## About
The Light is a modern Application designed to make managing your business simple and efficient.
## Website
 https://thelightui.web.app/




Powerful CRM & Business Management

Customer & Lead Management
Manage customers, leads, vendors, and employees in one place. Search, filter, assign salespeople, track follow-ups, and keep detailed customer histories.

Sales Pipeline & Jobs
Visual Kanban pipeline, job tracking, callbacks, sales targets, and real-time progress from lead to completed job.

Analytics & Reporting
Track revenue, leads, conversions, salesperson performance, deal size, and pipeline value with interactive dashboards, charts, reports, and revenue forecasting.

Communication & Outreach
Built-in team messaging, bulk Email/SMS, personalized outreach, and automated follow-up sequences to keep customers moving through your sales process.

Scheduling & Calendar
Manage appointments, meetings, reminders, and team schedules with calendar views and iOS calendar integration.

Financial Management
Create professional invoices and quotes, track expenses, monitor commissions, manage payments, and set financial goals.

Data & Operations
Import customers from CSV, perform bulk updates, track company activity, find duplicate records, and visualize customer distribution by location.

Maps & Location
Google Maps integration with directions, saved locations, geofencing, route information, distance, and ETA.

Productivity Tools
Personal to-do lists, global search, reminders, notifications, and easy access to customer information.

Administration & Security
Manage company settings, user profiles, authentication, and account preferences with secure Firebase-powered access.

Everything Your Team Needs in One Place

TheLight brings CRM, sales, communication, scheduling, financial management, reporting, and productivity together in one powerful platform—helping your team stay organized, close more business, and grow.
